# 2026 Recap: 5 Largest Office Leases in Austin, Texas

*Draft, 2026-11-16. Covers deals through AQUILA's 3Q 2026 Quarterly Report plus fourth-quarter deals announced publicly through mid-November. Refresh against the 4Q 2026 report in January.*

Austin office leasing in 2026 was a flight-to-quality story. The largest deals went to new or recently delivered towers downtown and along the Parmer and MoPac corridors. Several long-vacant trophy buildings finally landed anchor tenants.

## 1. Jackson Walker L.L.P.: The Republic, CBD

**117,038 SF, new lease, signed August 2026.** The law firm is relocating from 100 Congress to The Republic at 401 W. 4th St. Combined with Virtu Financial's 54,288 SF lease signed the same month, the deals pushed the tower to about 73% leased.

## 2. Sublease: Power Plant Building (Seaholm), CBD

**112,297 SF, sublease, signed March 2026.** The full block athenahealth had been marketing at 800 W. Cesar Chavez St. was subleased in one transaction. It was the largest contiguous sublease block left downtown, and its absorption was a meaningful step in working down Austin's sublease overhang.

## 3. Electronic Arts: 7700 Parmer, Northwest

**102,373 SF, renewal, signed April 2026.** EA renewed across Buildings A–D at 7700 W. Parmer Ln., keeping one of the larger tech campuses in Northwest Austin occupied.

## 4. SolarWinds: Zilker Point, South Lamar

**About 100,000 SF, new headquarters lease (4Q 2026).** SolarWinds will move its headquarters from Southwest Parkway to Zilker Point, 218 S. Lamar Blvd., in fall 2027. It is taking a little over half of a tower that had sat empty since it was completed in 2024.

## 5. Tesla: 717 Parmer, Northeast

**82,251 SF, new lease, signed July 2026.** Tesla added office space at 717 E. Parmer Ln., a short drive from its North Austin industrial operations.

## Just missing the list

- **Qualcomm Technologies**: 65,628 SF sublease at Parmer 4.1 (July).
- **Virtu Financial**: 54,288 SF new lease at The Republic (August).
- **Capital Area Council of Governments**: 52,650 SF at Monterey Oaks, Building A (April).
- **Renesas**: 48,908 SF at 3700 San Clemente (June), plus two further 23,180 SF leases in the same building.

## What it means

Three of the top five deals were tenants moving into newer, higher-quality space: The Republic, Zilker Point and 717 Parmer. A fourth took a large, high-quality sublease block downtown. Even with overall vacancy still elevated, the best buildings are where the large tenants went.

*Source: AQUILA Quarterly Report lease grids (1Q–3Q 2026), CoStar, Austin Business Journal. Square footage is as reported at signing.*
