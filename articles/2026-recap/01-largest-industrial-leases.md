# 2026 Recap: 5 Largest Industrial Leases Signed in Austin

*Draft, 2026-11-16. Covers deals through AQUILA's 3Q 2026 Quarterly Report plus fourth-quarter deals announced publicly through mid-November. Refresh against the 4Q 2026 report in January.*

Austin's industrial market spent 2026 proving that big-box demand never really left. The year's largest leases were not traditional distribution deals. They came from advanced manufacturers, aerospace and AI hardware companies, and they were concentrated along the SH-130 and Williamson County corridors, where new, large-format buildings were still available.

Here are the five largest industrial leases signed in the Austin area in 2026.

## 1. Blue Origin: Hutto Crossing (Constellation Park), Hutto

**About 1.3 million SF across a build-to-suit campus.** Blue Origin confirmed in October that it is coming to Hutto with Constellation Park, an R&D, manufacturing, warehouse and logistics campus at Hutto Crossing developed by a joint venture of Velocis, Ironwood Realty Partners and MBK Industrial Properties. The company puts its investment at more than $550 million and up to 2,000 jobs over ten years. It is the largest industrial commitment in the market this year by a wide margin.

## 2. Tesla: Austin Hills Commerce Center, Building 6, Northeast Austin

**681,259 SF, new lease, signed May 2026.** Tesla took all of Building 6 at 11801 Decker Lake Road, adding to its footprint near the Giga Texas campus.

## 3. Baer Manufacturing: Crosspoint Industrial Park, Building 5, Georgetown

**606,000 SF, new lease, signed February 2026.** Baer Manufacturing leased Building 5 at 4805 N. Interstate 35 in Williamson County, the year's largest manufacturing lease by a company new to the market. It also leased 73,510 SF in neighboring Building 3.

## 4. Tesla: Mustang Ridge Business Park, Building 2, Southeast Austin

**538,720 SF, new lease, signed July 2026.** This was Tesla's second major industrial lease of the year, at Hwy. 183 and FM 1327. Together the two deals total about 1.2 million SF.

## 5. ZT Systems: GTX Logistics Park, Building 2, Georgetown

**412,470 SF, new lease, signed March 2026.** ZT Systems, the AI data center infrastructure manufacturer, leased Building 2 at 600 FM 972. It is one of several AI hardware deals that drove Williamson County demand this year.

## Just missing the list

- **Total Site Solutions**: 388,000 SF at Berry Creek Business Park, Georgetown (seven-year lease announced in October; one building now, a second in 2027).
- **Flex Ltd.**: 310,366 SF at Blue Springs Business Park, Building 2, Georgetown (July). Flex also renewed 144,000 SF at Springbrook Corporate Center.
- **SoftBank**: 280,000 SF at Hutto Crossing, Building 3.
- **Four Hands**: 235,740 SF at Airport Logistics Center, Building 1 (August). With this deal the furniture maker now occupies the entire 806,229 SF, two-building property.

## What it means

Four of the five largest leases went to manufacturers, not logistics users. Williamson County (Georgetown and Hutto) captured three of the top five. For landlords with large, power-ready buildings north and east of the city, 2026 was a strong leasing year even as smaller-bay vacancy stayed elevated.

*Source: AQUILA Quarterly Report lease grids (1Q–3Q 2026), CoStar, Austin Business Journal. Square footage is as reported at signing.*
