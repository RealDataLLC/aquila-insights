*Update plan, 2026-11-16, for the Learning Center article first posted August 15, 2025. The ranked table needs a fresh CoStar tenant pull (office tenants in the Austin MSA, 150,000+ SF) after year-end. Terminal does not hold MSA-wide office tenant data.*

## Fixes to make in the current table

1. **Duplicate rows.** Ranks 53–57 repeat ranks 48–52 (Atlassian, PayPal, AT&T Labs, Dell Pediatric Research Institute, State Bar of Texas). Delete them; the list is really 52 companies.
2. **City of Austin appears twice** (rank 20, 438,766 SF, and rank 46, "City Of Austin," 167,537 SF). This is a casing difference in the CoStar export. Combine the two rows (about 606,303 SF), which moves the City up the list.
3. **Retitle or restate the method.** The article ranks occupiers by office square footage, not by employees. Say so in the intro ("ranked by office space occupied"), because readers will expect a headcount ranking.
