# Update: What Are the Largest Companies in Austin, Texas Today?

*Updated draft, 2026-11-16, for the Learning Center article first posted August 15, 2025. Data: CoStar tenant locations, Austin market (MSA), office space use, locations of 150,000+ SF, pulled November 2026. Totals add each company's qualifying locations, the same method as the 2025 list. The August 2025 source export (CoStar, pulled 2025-08-15) used the same filters. "Office" means CoStar's Space Use = Office, whatever the building type, which is why NXP's space in an industrial building counted last year.*

## Suggested intro and method note

Apple, Dell, IBM and Meta have long dominated Austin's office headlines, but 2026 reshuffled the list. NXP, last year's #1, is leaving its Oak Hill campus. SoftBank's SB Energy bought the former 3M campus outright. Google and Meta gave back large blocks of space.

*As of November 2026, this ranking shows the largest office occupiers in the Austin MSA. It counts every company with at least one office space of 150,000 square feet or more, ranked by the total square footage of those spaces. Data is sourced from CoStar.*

## What changed since August 2025

These come from comparing this export row by row with the August 2025 export (same filters).

- **NXP Semiconductors (was #1, 2,435,810 SF) is off the list, but it hasn't moved yet.** CoStar has removed NXP from the Oak Hill campus (6501 William Cannon Dr.), which NXP listed for sale with CBRE in December 2025. Press confirms the February 2026 lease of both Champion Office Park buildings. A $30 million renovation there is due to finish in March 2027, so NXP is still at Oak Hill today, and no sale of the campus has been reported. **Decision:** keep NXP on the list with a note that it moves in 2027, or drop it and say why. The new HQ won't qualify on its own, because neither building's space reaches 150,000 SF.
- **Google fell from #6 to #27** (1,193,921 → 271,321 SF). Its 738,000 SF at Sail Tower and 151,998 SF at the Offices at Saltillo are no longer on record, and 500 W. 2nd St. went from 303,923 to 271,321 SF.
- **Austin Community College's drop (#5 to #19, 1,216,000 → 354,722 SF) is a CoStar data gap, not a real change.** CoStar dropped the 1,016,000 SF Highland campus record at 6001 Airport Blvd., but ACC still runs the campus: Highland Phase 2 won a 2026 ULI Americas Award for Excellence. The only announced exit is the Highland Business Center (Building 8000), and that isn't until fall 2028. **Recommendation:** count the Highland record, which puts ACC at about 1,370,722 SF and #3, or leave ACC out until CoStar fixes it. Don't publish it at #19. Its 2026 Bergstrom Tech Center purchase (560,381 SF) isn't in CoStar either.
- **Samsung fell from #8 to #14** (991,207 → 499,998 SF). CoStar now records Samsung's Taylor office building at 499,998 SF, down from 800,000. The 191,207 SF it held at 15700 Long Vista Dr. now belongs to Allen Control Systems, which signed for that space in August 2026.
- **Meta fell to 833,644 SF.** IBM now holds Meta's former 320,095 SF at Domain 12. Sixth and Guadalupe (582,376 SF) and Third + Shoal (251,268 SF) remain.
- **Indeed fell to 520,494 SF** (from 703,937) after its 183,911 SF at Domain Gateway dropped off. Press confirms Indeed has been subleasing Domain Gateway since 2023: subtenants include Dun & Bradstreet and Zonda, and an e-commerce company took about 108,000 SF in 4Q 2025. Indeed's lease there runs to February 2028. Indeed also listed 118,185 SF at Domain Tower for sublease in October 2025, and CoStar reported in November 2026 that it is looking to exit Indeed Tower. Its two remaining spaces (Domain Tower 309,883 SF and Indeed Tower 210,611 SF) may not survive the next refresh. **Intel** fell to 353,438 SF (from 388,430).
- **New this year:** SB Energy (bought Highpoint 2222, #4), The Senate of Texas, Austin Energy (now listed separately from the City of Austin), BillionToOne and Baylor Scott & White Quality Alliance.
- **No longer have a 150,000+ SF office space:**
  - Texas Facilities Commission: George H.W. Bush State Office Building
  - Progressive: Met Center 15
  - eBay: 7700 Parmer D
  - Vista Equity Partners: The Republic
  - Silicon Labs: 417 W. 2nd St.
  - Atlassian: Centro
  - PayPal: Domain Tower 2

## Corrections carried over from the 2025 article

- The 2025 table repeated ranks 48–52 as 53–57. The new table has no repeats.
- City of Austin was listed twice in 2025 because of a casing difference ("City Of Austin"). Its spaces are combined here.
- The 2025 method note said "in a single building," but the totals combined multiple buildings. The method note above fixes that.
- **The Department of Assistive and Rehabilitative Services was abolished in 2016**, but CoStar still lists it at 6101 E. Oltorf St. Drop it, or relabel the space as HHSC if CoStar confirms.
- **To verify before publishing:**
  - "Round Rock CCTTI" (9900 Spectrum Dr.) isn't a recognizable name.
  - The City of Kyle's 450,000 SF is the Kyle Public Safety Center.
