# 2026 Recap: 5 Largest Office Building Sales in Austin, Texas

*Draft, 2026-11-16. Covers sales through AQUILA's 3Q 2026 Quarterly Report. Refresh against the 4Q 2026 report in January. Multi-building trades are combined and ranked by total square footage.*

After two quiet years, Austin office sales picked back up in 2026. The buyers were a mix: a data center developer, a community college, local private capital and, downtown, buyers paying well below replacement cost for Class A towers. Many of the year's biggest office sales were bought to be repositioned or put to a new use rather than held as traditional office investments.

## 1. Highpoint 2222: SB Energy

**About 1.18 million SF, $285.6 million (closed March 2026), Northwest.** SB Energy, a SoftBank Group affiliate, bought the former 3M campus at 6801 River Place Blvd. from Karlin Real Estate. It is converting the 107-acre site into an R&D and data center campus. This was the year's largest office sale by both size and price.

## 2. Bergstrom Tech Center, Buildings 310 and 312: Austin Community College

**560,381 SF across two buildings (2Q 2026), Southeast.** Austin Community College bought Buildings 310 (351,722 SF) and 312 (208,659 SF) from Boyd Watterson Global, adding a major campus near the airport.

## 3. One Eleven: Canyon Creek Real Estate

**519,959 SF, $208 million, about $400/SF (3Q 2026), CBD.** Canyon Creek Real Estate bought the downtown tower at 111 Congress from Cousins Properties. It was the largest downtown office sale of the year.

## 4. The Campus at the Arboretum: PacVentures

**319,339 SF across five buildings (2Q 2026), Northwest.** PacVentures bought Buildings I–V at The Campus at the Arboretum from Vanderbilt Office Properties and Starwood Capital Group.

## 5. 515 Congress: Palmatum Ventures, AQUILA Commercial and Glendon Capital

**267,956 SF (3Q 2026), CBD.** A partnership of Palmatum Ventures, AQUILA Commercial and Glendon Capital bought 515 Congress from KBS REIT III.

## Also notable

- **405 Colorado** (206,000 SF, CBD): Hines Global Income Trust bought it from Brandywine Realty Trust for $151 million, about $733/SF. It was the year's highest price per square foot for a large Austin office building.
- **823 Congress Ave.** (189,140 SF, CBD): Brick Row bought it from DRA Advisors and Pillar Commercial.
- **Bridgepoint Plaza I and II** (177,973 SF, Northwest): Continental General bought both buildings from Spire Realty Group.
- **Research Park Plaza V** (172,854 SF, Northwest): Onward Investors bought it from Cousins Properties for $42 million, about $243/SF.
- **Lavaca Plaza** (122,041 SF, CBD): Riverside bought it from Unico in September.

## What it means

Pricing in 2026 spanned a wide range. 405 Colorado traded above $700/SF, while several older suburban buildings sold well under $250/SF. Pricing came down to building quality and to what the buyer planned to do with the property. Two of the top five buyers, SB Energy and Austin Community College, bought office buildings to use for something other than leased office space.

*Source: AQUILA Quarterly Report sales grids (1Q–3Q 2026), CoStar, Austin Business Journal. Prices are shown only where publicly reported.*
